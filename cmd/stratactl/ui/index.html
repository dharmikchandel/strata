    What do these mean?
    Segment
    One immutable file holding a batch of log lines, with its own index and time range.
    Skipped by time range
    The segment's earliest and latest line fall outside the range you asked for, so it was ruled out from a small catalogue without being opened.
    Skipped by bloom filter
    A compact summary of every word in the segment said your word is definitely not in it. It can wrongly say "maybe" (costing one wasted read), never wrongly say "no".
    Read
    Downloaded and searched. This is the only cost that matters. Every segment is one bar in the strip, oldest on the left; a tall bar was read, a short one was skipped.
